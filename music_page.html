A Musical Reflection
This proposal draws inspiration from Yu Zhou Chang Wan, a traditional Chinese musical piece depicting fishermen gently making their way home by boat at the end of a day's work.

Traditionally associated with the Shandong region of China, the piece evokes a quiet scene of life along the water. Although originating from a different landscape, its essence resonates particularly strongly here.
